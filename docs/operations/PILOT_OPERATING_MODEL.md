# Controlled pilot operating model

Recommended first scope: one institutional buyer, one commodity, five to ten
farmers, one coordinator, one fulfilment node, and four to six weeks.

Pilot configuration should record name, organization, commodity, dates,
programme, participating farmers, coordinator, baseline definitions, targets,
status, and provenance for each baseline. Until actual events exist, every
outcome reads **NO MEASURED RESULT YET**.

Measured KPIs are derived only from recorded events and physical records:
required/covered quantity, shortage events, successful recoveries, unresolved
shortfall, recovery time, emergency procurement events, received/accepted/
rejected quantity, farmer response time and confirmation rate, data freshness,
and coordinator interventions. Monetary savings remain **NOT MEASURED** unless
an approved baseline and counterfactual exist.

## Operating rhythm

1. Coordinator reviews stale and expiring observations daily.
2. Farmers respond through their preferred governed channel.
3. Procurement reviews Decision Inbox cases requiring commercial authority.
4. Warehouse records receipt and inspection as separate facts.
5. Buyer confirmation and reconciliation close the trade.
6. Weekly review reports recorded outcomes, missing data, exceptions, and
   protocol deviations without manufacturing success.

# KoroFarm 2.0 direction

KoroFarm — Agricultural Intelligence & Coordination Infrastructure for the Caribbean.

Understand production. Anticipate risk. Coordinate action. Strengthen economic resilience.

This sprint adds public national statistical intelligence to the existing supply-assurance application. Homepage introductory copy, page metadata and navigation were lightly updated; established operational routes and entity names remain unchanged. Intelligence Lab has its own `/intelligence-lab` entry alongside Scenario Lab, Network and Analytics.

Observed national statistics, statistical predictions, recommendations, authorized actions and verified physical outcomes remain distinct. The current operational engine coordinates commitments, standby, disruption recovery, inspection, acceptance and traceability. A national production forecast does not initiate operational recovery.

Value Pathways is displayed once as **Concept / Planned**: future verified processing capacity, market prices, quality and logistics data could support comparing fresh sales, storage and processing. No processing facilities, optimizer, profitability figures or action buttons were invented. Agricultural intelligence remains broader than agro-processing.

Next milestone: establish a Ministry data partnership to resolve the 2016 Q3 conflict, obtain publication/revision vintages and newer quarters, and run timestamped prospective evaluations before claiming real-time predictive advantage. Add independent demand, usable stock and import data only if pursuing a separate shortage-risk model.
